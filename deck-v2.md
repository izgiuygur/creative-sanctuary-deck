# Creative's Sanctuary — Deck v2

52 cards, 13 per element. Agreed 2026-10-13.

**Voice:** a playful trickster friend talking straight at you, half joke, half real advice. Sentence structure varies card to card.

**Image rule:** one isolated subject, no scene, no punchline in the picture. Dense engraved line work in a single ink on a pale tinted ground.

† = darker card
